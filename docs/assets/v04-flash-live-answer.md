# How does fastMRI+ extend fastMRI, and does this evidence establish diagnostic accuracy?

fastMRI+ extends fastMRI by adding expert pathology annotations to knee and brain images from fastMRI, where the annotations include bounding boxes and study-level labels, thereby extending the original reconstruction resource with information about visible pathology [PMC:PMC8983757]. The fastMRI+ labels support imaging research, but this fixture does not establish diagnostic accuracy and does not provide individual treatment recommendations [PMC:PMC8983757].

Research demonstration; not clinical advice.
